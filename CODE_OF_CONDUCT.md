# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Block Language to be a welcoming, practical, and technically rigorous
project. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good
intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Welcome different experience levels and language backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information.
- Follow the security reporting process for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
sexualized conduct, and publishing private information without consent are not
acceptable.

Reports may be made privately through the maintainer contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content that
violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the
project safe.
